How it works

Inscriptions are worn, abbreviated and written without spaces. Stelescan is built for exactly that.

  1. Capture

    Take a photo of a stone, a tablet or a monument. The app helps you get a clear, straight shot.

  2. Recognise

    It finds the text, works out the script and language, and reads the signs, even damaged ones.

  3. Transcribe

    You see the original text in a clean form, with abbreviations spelled out and gaps marked.

  4. Translate

    A translation in your language, plus a short note on what the inscription is and why it matters.
